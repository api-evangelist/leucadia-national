# Leucadia National (leucadia-national)
Leucadia National Corporation was a diversified holding company engaged in a variety of businesses, including investment banking, beef processing, manufacturing, telecommunications, and real estate. In 2018 it was renamed Jefferies Financial Group following the merger with Jefferies Group LLC.
